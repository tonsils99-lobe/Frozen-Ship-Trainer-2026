## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Frozen Ship**. This trainer supports expeditions beyond the ship and the management systems that keep the crew alive. It provides control over player survival values, resources, crew conditions, crafting, research, and expedition-related progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to focus on Frozen Ship’s story, exploration, crafting, research, trading, and mobile-city management without the usual survival pressure.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Leave the ship with the crew at the start of the workday and explore the surrounding frozen world in first person. Gather materials, investigate locations, complete tasks, and return before evening. Cold, hunger, distance, and time should be monitored during every expedition.

### Tools & Inventory

Use collected materials and discovered items to support the crew, craft equipment, trade with settlements, and maintain the ship. The trainer’s resource options can reduce the need to repeatedly search for basic supplies, but important quest items should still be collected normally.

### World & Environment

Frozen Ship uses a procedurally generated world containing abandoned settlements, resources, quests, traders, wildlife, and changing events. Back aboard the ship, assign crew members, construct and upgrade facilities, research technologies, enact laws, and manage the expedition’s limited supplies.

### Combat & Weapons

Dangerous wildlife and other threats can appear during exploration and hunting. Keep suitable equipment ready, avoid prolonged exposure, and return to the ship when conditions become unsafe. The trainer can provide protection from damage and preserve selected hunting supplies.
